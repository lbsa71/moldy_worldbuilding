# Journey prototype: canon and consequence cards

Updated 7 October 2026. Story edition: `journey-2026-10-07-v2`. First playable consequence experiment, separate from the accepted chapter. Human response and duration remain untested. Version 2 shortened the first draft after feedback about long scrolling passages. The latest correction groups that same prose into fuller pages: the user found paragraph-sized cards too fragmented and prioritizes desktop reading. This changes presentation, not the story or save edition. The original 8–12 minute hypothesis remains a playtest question, not a word quota or a claim about this edition.

## Person, task and stable truth

Mara repaired library books. She is precise about materials, impatient with sentimental inscriptions, fond of badly behaved readers, and proud of making damaged things usable. She is fading; no diagnosis or explanation of the shore is supplied. Her sister Rosa died after a hospital stay. Mara wants to leave her daughter Ada a usable account of her last visit, without leaving Ada the job of deciding whether Mara was a good sister.

Mara remembers leaving the room to buy tea, Rosa asking her to stop fussing, and rain on her sleeve. A receipt preserved in a book establishes a purchase at 21:14. It does **not** establish when Mara left or returned, whether someone else visited, whether Rosa was awake, or when Rosa died. The chip and cup orientation establish a particular repeated custom, not proof of uninterrupted attendance. These facts never change between routes. Mara's conviction that Rosa knew she was loved is a belief; the game never upgrades it into verified evidence.

The books are Mara's repair manual, Rosa's paperback and a notebook used for Ada's message. The stage renders them together as one presence group: opening and reading happen in narration; no animated page, writing hand or visible inscription is claimed. Disregarding the books closes documentary and written-message opportunities. Keeping all three closed also makes the account spoken; retaining an object does not override a promise about its use. The receipt cannot subsequently be quoted from an absent book. Disregarding the cup closes the opportunity to teach its handling by demonstration. Recalled facts remain known after an object is released; physical actions require the object.

The chair and lamp establish an encounter. Books enter when Mara names the work they can do; the curtain and rail enter when the hospital memory is willingly approached. A prop never disappears because the player is slow, silent, chooses another topic, or fails an invisible empathy test. Explicit dismissal is allowed to disappoint Mara without changing the shore's brightness or damage. At the final departure every foreground prop leaves in every ending, including the lamp. The common empty composition measures neither success nor affection.

## Finite state

No virtue total. `approach` records the first commitment; `books` is open/closed/gone; `cup_kept` records explicit preservation or dismissal; `boundary` is final/revisit; `repair` is acknowledge/maintain/limit; `draft` records the actual offered account; `readback` records one expressive attention choice; `departure` records the final act. State is set by choices, never elapsed time. `book_dismissed` and `cup_dismissed` remain true even at the universal empty ending, distinguishing an earlier loss from a completed encounter.

| J1 Approach / major | Mara asks for an account Ada can live with. | J2 Books / major | Mara identifies a repair manual, Rosa's book and the notebook. | J4 Agreement / major | Mara asks to stop investigating. | J5 Disagreement / major | Mara challenges what the player's approach has made of her. | J6 Account / major | Make the practical task concrete: a bounded document, an explicitly owned belief, an ordinary custom, an honest statement of uncertainty or an account of disagreement. A verbal account is never falsely described as a physical letter when the books were dismissed. |

## Verification and research limits

`src/ink/journey.test.ts` compiles the authored story, enumerates every acyclic choice sequence in this edition, compares saved and restored dialogue at each reached state, checks durable prop loss, and asserts the common ending composition. Targeted counterfactuals inspect later available actions rather than merely checking variables. Enumeration proves only those software properties for this finite script and runtime; it does not prove emotional response, readable pacing, engagement, or replay motivation.

The [reproducible route report](evidence/journey-01-routes.json) records **1,344 complete choice sequences**, **2,191 reached prefixes**, **13 stable scene IDs**, and **eight decisions on every route**, unchanged from version 1. Each ending is reachable through 448 sequences. Seven distinct accounts remain available; the four-option menus retain their alternatives. Shared scenes with conditional text keep one ID rather than inflating novelty.

Version 2 contains **744–829 visible words per route**, including every displayed choice label, and **35 authored paragraphs**. The current helper groups them into **nine base prose cards per route**; browser reflow and separate decision pages can add pages. The superseded policy showed those paragraphs as 35 short cards. At an assumed 200 words/minute the unchanged text is approximately 3.7–4.2 minutes of reading before deliberation and navigation, not observed playtime. Do not add words, forced waits or unnecessary interactions to recover an earlier duration estimate.

The desktop reading target is roughly **70–100 words together**. The shared helper caps a group at **100 whitespace-delimited words and 660 characters**, preserving paragraph breaks and source offsets. Across every reachable variant, the observed group maxima are **93 words, 535 characters and four paragraphs**. Choice labels remain bounded at eight words/48 characters, headings at 30 characters. Paragraph length is descriptive, not a page-break rule. Desktop browser checks establish actual fit; smaller screens retain adaptive fitting but no longer form a mandatory release gate.

Books and remembered rain remain named at beat entry, so their scene and sound cues do not anticipate a later Continue. Continue and Back navigate the same passage; they neither choose a branch, count as meaningful decisions, replay cues nor change consent or prop facts. The final paragraph stays with its responses where space permits, and earlier context remains available through Back. The [reading contract](dialogue-card-contract.md) distinguishes these invariants, current grouping and the preserved historical fit evidence.

The companion's resistance and the wording of deliberate dismissal need human review through [pilot 01](playtests/narrative-pilot-01.md), especially H1, H2, H4 and H6. The complete 30–45 minute game, matched visual control and preserved historical replay forks remain subsequent gates in the [production plan](narrative-experiments.md).
