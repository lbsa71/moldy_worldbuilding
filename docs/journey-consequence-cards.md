# Journey prototype: canon and consequence cards

7 October 2026. Story edition: `journey-2026-10-07-v2`. First playable consequence experiment, separate from the accepted chapter. Human response and duration remain untested. Version 2 responds to player feedback that long scrolling cards break immersion: prose is deliberately shorter, with presentation pages inside the same eight Ink decisions. The original 8–12 minute hypothesis is a playtest question, not a word quota or a claim about this edition.

## Person, task and stable truth

Mara repaired library books. She is precise about materials, impatient with sentimental inscriptions, fond of badly behaved readers, and proud of making damaged things usable. She is fading; no diagnosis or explanation of the shore is supplied. Her sister Rosa died after a hospital stay. Mara wants to leave her daughter Ada a usable account of her last visit, without leaving Ada the job of deciding whether Mara was a good sister.

Mara remembers leaving the room to buy tea, Rosa asking her to stop fussing, and rain on her sleeve. A receipt preserved in a book establishes a purchase at 21:14. It does **not** establish when Mara left or returned, whether someone else visited, whether Rosa was awake, or when Rosa died. The chip and cup orientation establish a particular repeated custom, not proof of uninterrupted attendance. These facts never change between routes. Mara's conviction that Rosa knew she was loved is a belief; the game never upgrades it into verified evidence.

The books are Mara's repair manual, Rosa's paperback and a notebook used for Ada's message. The stage renders them together as one presence group: opening and reading happen in narration; no animated page, writing hand or visible inscription is claimed. Disregarding the books closes documentary and written-message opportunities. Keeping all three closed also makes the account spoken; retaining an object does not override a promise about its use. The receipt cannot subsequently be quoted from an absent book. Disregarding the cup closes the opportunity to teach its handling by demonstration. Recalled facts remain known after an object is released; physical actions require the object.

The chair and lamp establish an encounter. Books enter when Mara names the work they can do; the curtain and rail enter when the hospital memory is willingly approached. A prop never disappears because the player is slow, silent, chooses another topic, or fails an invisible empathy test. Explicit dismissal is allowed to disappoint Mara without changing the shore's brightness or damage. At the final departure every foreground prop leaves in every ending, including the lamp. The common empty composition measures neither success nor affection.

## Finite state

No virtue total. `approach` records the first commitment; `books` is open/closed/gone; `cup_kept` records explicit preservation or dismissal; `boundary` is final/revisit; `repair` is acknowledge/maintain/limit; `draft` records the actual offered account; `readback` records one expressive attention choice; `departure` records the final act. State is set by choices, never elapsed time. `book_dismissed` and `cup_dismissed` remain true even at the universal empty ending, distinguishing an earlier loss from a completed encounter.

## Consequence cards

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| J1 Approach / major | Mara asks for an account Ada can live with. Reassurance can relieve a burden but risks claiming knowledge the player does not have; inquiry can establish a limited fact but cannot answer the central question; practical presence can meet an immediate need while leaving her request unanswered. | Three substantive approach episodes; J6 offers the particular work begun by that approach, subject to later agreements and surviving props. | J5 can acknowledge the approach's overreach, retain a defensible position, or negotiate its limit. It cannot retroactively turn an assurance into evidence. |
| J2 Books / major | Mara identifies a repair manual, Rosa's book and the notebook. Keep them available to examine, promise to retain all three closed, or explicitly dismiss documentary judgement and let them leave. | At J6 only books retained for possible reading under a revisitable agreement support a quoted receipt. Closed books support an unread offer, with the account spoken; dismissed books support neither physical act. All later stage cues preserve presence or absence. | Closed books can be discussed without opening. Dismissed books never return. Verbal testimony and admitting uncertainty remain possible. |
| J3 Cup / major | Mara offers a particular custom: turn the chipped side away. Preserve that ordinary knowledge or explicitly release the cup as insufficient to answer the larger question. | The later practical message can demonstrate the cup only when kept; after dismissal a different practical act uses the agreed pause instead. | Acknowledging a hurt does not restore the prop. The custom can still be remembered, but no absent cup can be handled. |
| J4 Agreement / major | Mara asks to stop investigating. Make a firm promise to leave the last evening alone for this encounter, or reserve the possibility of asking again while promising not to investigate without consent. | A firm promise removes later receipt-reading even when books are present; a revisitable agreement and an acknowledged limit can receive fresh permission. J6 action labels state the actual available act. The separate J2 promise to keep books closed can never be overridden. | There is no silent promise override. A maintained disagreement can continue without reopening evidence. |
| J5 Disagreement / major | Mara challenges what the player's approach has made of her. Acknowledge the overreach, maintain a limited claim, or hold a personal boundary and offer presence. | J6 distinguishes joint examination after explicit fresh permission, Mara's own belief, and an account of disagreement. These permit different J8 acts: passing on the bounded receipt account, two voices without reconciliation, or the honestly unfinished account. Tests execute those delayed choices, not only the coda's callbacks. | Acknowledgement changes what can be attempted together; it does not automatically earn forgiveness, restore discarded objects or reveal new truth. |
| J6 Account / major | Make the practical task concrete: a bounded document, an explicitly owned belief, an ordinary custom, an honest statement of uncertainty or an account of disagreement. The strongest-sounding claim is not always available. | J8 changes the available concrete departure acts according to the actual draft, its medium and the retained props. The departure two decisions later does not reset this work. | A player can choose not to leave the message in J8; earlier claims and costs remain in the coda. No final choice proves Rosa's experience. |
| J7 Readback / expressive | Mara asks whether to hear the agreed words once more or let them rest. | Changes the immediate shared moment and later acknowledgement, without opening a hidden superior ending. | Both are explicit attention choices. Idle time does nothing. This is not advertised as a plot hinge. |
| J8 Departure / terminal | Leave the agreed account for Ada, leave it with Mara without assigning Ada a task, or leave no message tonight. | Each ending executes that act and remembers approach, agreement, dismissed objects and repair. All three have exactly the same empty stage, wide framing, resolved mood, no rain and 2.4-second dissolve. | An unsigned or unshared account does not erase the conversation. A verbal account is never falsely described as a physical letter when the books were dismissed. |

## Verification and research limits

`src/ink/journey.test.ts` compiles the authored story, enumerates every acyclic choice sequence in this edition, compares saved and restored dialogue at each reached state, checks durable prop loss, and asserts the common ending composition. Targeted counterfactuals inspect later available actions rather than merely checking variables. Enumeration proves only those software properties for this finite script and runtime; it does not prove emotional response, readable pacing, engagement, or replay motivation.

The [reproducible route report](evidence/journey-01-routes.json) records **1,344 complete choice sequences**, **2,191 reached prefixes**, **13 stable scene IDs**, and **eight decisions on every route**, unchanged from version 1. Each ending is reachable through 448 sequences. Seven distinct accounts remain available; the four-option menus retain their alternatives. Shared scenes with conditional text keep one ID rather than inflating novelty.

Version 2 contains **744–829 visible words per route**, including every displayed choice label, and **35 short authored prose cards**. At an assumed 200 words/minute this is approximately 3.7–4.2 minutes of reading before deliberation and page navigation, not observed playtime. Do not add words, forced waits or unnecessary interactions to recover the earlier duration estimate. Measure whether the shorter encounter is compelling before expanding it.

Every reachable prose card is at most **35 words and 220 characters**, every choice at most **eight words and 48 characters**, and every heading at most **30 characters**. The observed maxima are 31 prose words, 178 prose characters, eight choice words and 48 choice characters. Every Ink beat contains two to four authored cards. Dynamic fitting may split a card further or separate its choices on smaller viewports or enlarged text. Books and the remembered rain are named on their entry cards so their scene and sound cues do not anticipate a later Continue. Continue and Back navigate these cards; they neither choose a story branch nor count as meaningful decisions, replay scene cues, or change a consent or prop fact. The final card gives the immediate decision context; previous cards remain available through Back. Automated checks enforce the content bounds, while real viewport checks establish whether the whole card fits without scrolling.

The companion's resistance and the wording of deliberate dismissal need human review through [pilot 01](playtests/narrative-pilot-01.md), especially H1, H2, H4 and H6. The complete 30–45 minute game, matched visual control and preserved historical replay forks remain subsequent gates in the [production plan](narrative-experiments.md).
